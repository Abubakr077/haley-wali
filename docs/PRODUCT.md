# Product and business context

## Brand

- Customer-facing name: **Haley Wali**.
- Planned domain: **haleywali.pk**.
- Market: Pakistan only.
- Currency: Pakistani rupees, shown as `PKR`.
- First payment method: **Cash on Delivery**.
- Nationwide delivery PKR is a Store Manager setting, default **PKR 250** until
  the owner changes it. An offer code can still make delivery free for one order.
- The packing allowance used for suggested selling price is separate from
  customer delivery and must not be treated as the same amount.
- The store must work well for customers shopping from a mobile phone.

The old working name was Noor Luxe. New customer-facing work must use Haley
Wali. The old Instagram link has been removed until the final Haley Wali account
is confirmed.

## What the store sells

There are exactly two main customer collections:

### HW Exclusive

Haley Wali's own customised articles. These are made or designed for Haley Wali
and are not reseller stock. An exclusive article may still be Pret or
unstitched, but `HW Exclusive` is its main collection.

### Branded

Articles bought from the wholesale market or another supplier and sold by Haley
Wali. This includes Sapphire, Saya, Alkaram, other brands available in the
market, and imported supplier stock.

`Pret` and `Unstitched` are article types inside Branded, not separate main
collections. The source supplier must be stored internally but must not be named
on the customer website. Original brand names such as Sapphire may be shown when
the article is genuinely from that brand.

Use the customer filter grouping `Other Brands` when a real Branded article is
manually sourced or imported but its original supplier or brand should not be
named. The article itself keeps the generic public label `Branded`; never
substitute the upstream supplier name.

## Product language

Use familiar Pakistan clothing-market wording:

- article, suit, stock, Pret, ready to wear, unstitched;
- 2 piece / 3 piece, shirt, trouser, dupatta;
- lawn, khaddar, linen, cotton and other actual fabric names;
- buying cost, selling price, margin, available sizes, out of stock;
- add to bag, Cash on Delivery, delivery, offer code, exchange and track order.

Avoid unusual retail jargon such as “curated resale.” Keep copy short and easy
to understand. Do not use inflated luxury claims that cannot be supported.

## Product goals

- A distinctive clothing-store look based on the selected Burberry-style
  reference, adapted to Haley Wali rather than copied literally.
- A complete frontend flow before advanced backend integrations.
- Low ongoing cost, with a developer-friendly codebase preferred over paid
  no-code tools.
- Owner control over every public article, price, stock value and order stage.
- Supplier imports that create private drafts and never publish automatically.
- Production may begin with an empty catalog. The owner or a helper signs in to
  Store Manager, adds an HW Exclusive article, completes its information and
  stock, then publishes it when ready.

## Out of scope for the current stage

- Customer accounts and login.
- Online card payment.
- Automatic courier booking.
- A public, unauthenticated production admin panel.
- Automatic publication or automatic price changes from supplier data.
